# Social Interaction Rules Supplement (SRD 5.2.1 Compatible)

## Part 1: Universal Interaction Rules

When a player character interacts with an NPC or monster from the *System Reference Document 5.2.1*, the Dungeon Master may consult the Universal Social Interaction Table to determine baseline responses and skill interactions.

### Universal Social Interaction Table
| Creature Type | Default Attitude | Preferred Social Skill | Positive Trigger (DC -2 / Advantage) | Negative Trigger (DC +5 / Auto-Fail) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aberration** | Hostile | **Deception** | Offering lost lore or forbidden historical knowledge. | Invoking the names of modern gods or planar entities. |
| **Beast** | Indifferent | **Animal Handling** | Offering fresh food; moving slowly with weapons sheathed. | Cornering the beast or making sudden, deafening noises. |
| **Celestial** | Friendly | **Persuasion** | Honesty, acts of self-sacrifice, or defending innocents. | Attempting to lie (*Deception*) or threatening violence. |
| **Construct** | Hostile | None (Immune) | Providing specific maintenance commands or repair keys. | Damaging its guarded property or structures. |
| **Dragon** | Indifferent | **Intimidation** / **Persuasion** | Flattery, tribute offerings, or acknowledging their hierarchy. | Touching their hoard or showing blatant disrespect. |
| **Elemental** | Indifferent | **Persuasion** | Presenting pure elemental fuel (e.g., source of fire or rare minerals). | Using spells that control, bind, or banish elementals. |
| **Fey** | Indifferent | **Deception** / **Persuasion** | Engaging in playful riddles, gift-giving, or clever wit. | Breaking a sworn promise, contract, or formal oath. |
| **Fiend** | Hostile | **Deception** | Offering written contracts, collateral, or souls. | Appealing to their morality, mercy, or altruism. |
| **Giant** | Hostile | **Intimidation** | Demonstrating significant physical strength or tactical military logic. | Insulting their lineage, craft, or social standing. |
| **Humanoid** | Indifferent | **Persuasion** / **Deception** | Aligning with their faction's goals or trading currency/goods. | Threatening their family, home territory, or leadership. |
| **Monstrosity** | Hostile | **Animal Handling** | Throwing specialized food/minerals or giving them wide berths. | Blocking their clear path to prey or active hunting grounds. |
| **Ooze** | Hostile | None (Immune) | Luring it away with massive amounts of organic waste/acidic matter. | Damaging it with elements it naturally absorbs. |
| **Plant** | Hostile | None (Immune) | Presenting specialized fertilizers or altering light/water sources. | Introducing open fire, defoliants, or clear-cutting tools. |
| **Undead** | Hostile | **Intimidation** *(Intelligent only)* | Displaying symbols of dark authority or necromantic control. | Brandishing holy symbols, radiant magic, or life energy. |

### Language-Locked Negotiations (Rare & Exotic Dialects)
When a character uses a creature's native or ancestral language during social interaction, they gain **Advantage** on their Charisma check. Alternatively, if a creature is normally *Hostile*, speaking their native tongue can instantly shift their initial attitude to *Indifferent* for the duration of the conversation.

| Language Category | SRD 5.2.1 Examples | Social Interaction Mechanical Bonus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Native** *(e.g., Orc, Dwarvish, Elvish, Goblin)* | Orcs, Goblins, Kobolds, Dwarven clans | Shifts starting attitude from **Hostile to Indifferent**. Allows negotiation instead of immediate combat. |
| **Exotic/Planar** *(e.g., Abyssal, Celestial, Infernal, Primordial)* | Fiends, Celestials, Elementals | Bypasses traditional negotiation blockades. Drops the Influence DC by a **flat 5 points**, as planars respect the precision of their home tongues. |
| **Secret/Ancient** *(e.g., Deep Speech, Druidic, Thieves' Cant)* | Aberrations, Druids, Rogues, Underground Factions | Establishes immediate trust or fear. Grants **Advantage** on the check and prevents the creature from taking negative *Social Triggers* unless overtly attacked. |

## Part 2: Sample Factions

### 1. The Broken Ring (Outlaw & Mercenary Faction)
A ruthless syndicate of highwaymen, deserters, and opportunists operating out of abandoned border forts. They care nothing for politics, only profit and self-preservation.
* **Composition:** Primarily Humanoids (Bandits, Thugs, Scouts).
* **Baseline Attitude:** Hostile. They assume any armed group entering their territory is either a rival crew or a bounty hunting squad.
* **The Power Dynamic Rule:** If the party’s highest Strength or Charisma modifier is lower than the Bandit Captain's (+3), all *Persuasion* checks are made with **Disadvantage**. However, *Intimidation* or *Deception* checks gain a **+2 bonus** if the party displays visible trophies of defeated monsters or rivals.
* **Linguistic Alignment:** Speaking *Thieves' Cant* or *Goblin* bypasses the initial hostility, shifting the starting attitude directly to **Indifferent**.
* **Key Leverage:** Cartography detailing poorly guarded trade caravans, highly stable counterfeit coin operations, or crates of heavy armor and martial weaponry.

### 2. The Obsidian Scribes (Scholarly & Arcane Faction)
A secretive cabal of historians and mages dedicated to mapping the ley lines and unearthing ancient, pre-cataclysmic archives. They view mundane politics as a temporary distraction.
* **Composition:** Humanoids (Mages, Acolytes) occasionally employing bound Elementals.
* **Baseline Attitude:** Indifferent. They view adventurers as loud, clumsy variables who disrupt delicate research sites.
* **The Intellectual Merit Rule:** Before taking the Influence Action, a character must succeed on a **DC 13 Intelligence (Arcana or History)** check to articulate their request using proper arcane terminology. Succeeding on this check by 5 or more (DC 18+) instantly shifts the Scribes' attitude to **Friendly**. Failing this check causes the Scribes to ignore the party entirely.
* **Linguistic Alignment:** Invoking *Draconic* or *Primordial* commands respect, dropping the eventual Influence check DC by a **flat 5 points**.
* **Key Leverage:** Intact ancient spell scrolls, rare material components (such as diamonds or pristine monster organs), or the exact coordinates of unmapped planar rifts.

### 3. The Keepers of the Pale Flame (Zealot & Cultist Faction)
A doomsday sect worshiping a slumbering primordial entity. They believe the current civilizations are a disease that must be purged by fire to purify the world.
* **Composition:** Humanoids (Cultists, Cult Fanatics) acting alongside minor Fiends.
* **Baseline Attitude:** Hostile. Outsiders are viewed as fuel for their sacrificial pyres or direct threats to their grand prophecy.
* **The Dogmatic Wall Rule:** Standard *Persuasion* checks **fail automatically** against these zealots. Players must rely entirely on **Deception** to mimic their dogma, or **Intimidation** by projecting an aura of divine or cosmic wrath.
* **Linguistic Alignment:** Speaking *Abyssal* or *Infernal* shifts their baseline attitude to **Indifferent**, as they mistake the speaker for an emissary or handler sent by their dark patrons.
* **Key Leverage:** Stolen religious artifacts belonging to their sect, compromising blackmail on their High Priest, or forged astrological alignments proving that their deity demands the party be spared.
